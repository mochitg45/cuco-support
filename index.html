About the app

CUCO opens on your trip and shows what you can spend today. It also converts 166 currencies plus Bitcoin, gold and silver, and adds the things that actually come up on a trip: a rates list you choose yourself, exchange rate charts and alerts, a travel budget, a bill splitter, a cash wallet, and a card-versus-cash comparison. It needs no account.

Common questions

How current are the rates?

Fiat currency rates come from free public data and update about once a day. Bitcoin, gold and silver refresh roughly every sixty seconds while the app is open. Every screen shows the time its rates were published, so you can always see how fresh they are.

Why does my bank give me a different rate?

The rates in CUCO are mid-market reference rates. Banks, card issuers, airport kiosks and exchange counters all add a margin, so what you actually receive is lower. The "real rate after fees" field exists for exactly this: enter their percentage and see what you will be given. Do not rely on CUCO for trading or investment decisions.

Does it work offline?

Yes. CUCO caches the last rates it downloaded, so conversion keeps working with no connection. The rates are then as old as the last time you had signal, and the app says so rather than hiding it.

Why is there no history for some pairs?

The free historical data covers about 30 major currencies. Where real history is not available for a pair, CUCO says so instead of drawing a line it cannot support.

Where is my data kept?

On your device only. Your rates list, alerts, trips, expenses and settings never leave the phone, and there is no account or server copy. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

How do I remove the ads?

Remove ads is a one-time purchase in Tools → Settings. It hides the banner for good on that store account. If you reinstall, or move to a new phone with the same Google Play or Apple account, tap Restore purchases to bring it back.

What is the daily allowance?

CUCO takes your trip budget, subtracts what you spent before today, and divides the rest by the days left including today. What you have spent today is taken off that. The budget is kept in your home currency and shown in the local currency at today's rate, so the figure can move a little with the exchange rate. Trips without a budget or without an end date show what you spent today and your daily average instead.

How does Find trips in my calendar work?

CUCO only reads your calendar when you tap the button and after you allow it. It looks for flight and hotel events in the coming twelve months on your device and suggests trips from them. When you add one, CUCO saves that trip (its city, currency and dates) and the ids of the events it came from, so they are not suggested again. Nothing else from your calendar is stored, and nothing leaves your phone. You can turn calendar access off in your device settings at any time.

Alerts are not notifying me

Alerts are checked while CUCO is open, and notifications need permission. Check that notifications are allowed for CUCO in your device settings, and that battery optimization is not stopping the app from running.

Privacy

The full policy is on the privacy page. The short version: no account, no location, nothing uploaded to us. The app downloads exchange rates and shows ads through Google AdMob.
