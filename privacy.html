Privacy Policy — CUCO Currency & Travel

Last updated: 7 September 2026

CUCO ("the app") is developed and published by mochitg45. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

CUCO has no accounts and no server of its own. Everything you create in the app — your saved currency list, rate alerts, trips and expenses, and your settings — is stored only on your device. We cannot see it, and it is never uploaded to us. The app does two things that involve the network: it downloads exchange rates, and it shows advertisements.

Information the app stores on your device

The following stays on your phone and is never transmitted to us:

  • The currencies in your rates list, and their order
  • Your rate alerts and whether they have triggered
  • Your trips, expenses, categories, notes, and the exchange rate recorded with each expense
  • Your settings: home currency, chosen theme, and recent currency pairs
  • A cached copy of the most recent exchange rates, so the app works offline

Uninstalling CUCO deletes all of it. There is no server-side copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on request and no account to close.

Exchange rate data

To show current rates, the app requests publicly available data from:

  • open.er-api.com — fiat currency rates
  • CoinGecko (api.coingecko.com) — Bitcoin, gold and silver prices
  • Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev) — historical rates for the charts

These are ordinary web requests. They do not carry your name, your device identifiers, or anything you have entered in the app. As with any internet request, the provider necessarily receives your device's IP address and can see which currencies were requested. Each provider handles that under its own privacy policy.

Advertising

CUCO displays banner advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's advertising SDK collects information from your device, which typically includes your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general device information, and your interactions with the ads shown.

This collection is performed by Google, not by us, and it is governed by Google's own policies: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner sites and apps.

On Android you can limit this at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID. On iOS you control it through the App Tracking Transparency prompt and in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

We do not receive your personal information from Google. We see only aggregate reporting, such as how many ads were shown.

Permissions

  • Internet access — to download exchange rates and advertisements.
  • Notifications — only if you create a rate alert and choose to allow them. Notifications are generated on your device; no notification content is sent to a server. You may decline, and the rest of the app works normally.

CUCO does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or access to your files. It does not read or store any payment or banking details, and it cannot move money.

Children

CUCO is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Accuracy of rates

Exchange rates in CUCO are provided for reference only. They come from free public data, fiat rates update approximately once per day, and they will differ from the rate a bank, card issuer or exchange actually gives you. Do not rely on CUCO for financial, trading or investment decisions.

Changes to this policy
